Check a calibration tolerance

A balance needs a prior for its residual zero offset in milligrams. Calibration requires at least 90% probability within 2 mg of zero. Compare two fixed candidate laws centered at zero, both with a scale parameter of 1 mg.

using Credence, MeasureBase, Randombeliefs = (:offset => (Hard(:tolerance, Probability(-2, 2), AtLeast(0.9)),),)candidates = (    :offset => (        Candidate(:normal, Returns(StdNormal()), (NamedTuple(),)),        Candidate(:logistic, Returns(StdLogistic()), (NamedTuple(),)),    ),)problem = Elicitation(beliefs, candidates)report = fit(Xoshiro(101), problem)validation = validate(Xoshiro(102), report)belief_summary(validation)

The Normal assigns about 95% probability to the tolerance band. The Logistic assigns about 76%. Select the candidate that meets the requirement and record why.

choice = select(    report,    :offset,    (:normal, 1);    validation,    rationale="The Normal meets the zero-offset tolerance.",)prior = measure(choice)rand(Xoshiro(103), prior^4)

  1.3286282485395753

prior^4 is the product of four copies of the prior. Sampling from it gives four independent offsets.
