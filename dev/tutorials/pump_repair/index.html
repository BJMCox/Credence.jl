Compare families for a repair time

A maintenance engineer estimates a median of 2 hours for a pump repair and expects 90% of repairs to finish within 6 hours. Fit both a LogNormal and a Weibull.

using Credence, Distributions, Optimization, OptimizationOptimJL, Randomusing MeasureBase: logdensityofusing CairoMakieimport ForwardDiffbeliefs = (    :hours => (        Approximate(:median, Quantile(0.5), 2.0; scale=0.5),        Approximate(:upper, Quantile(0.9), 6.0; scale=1.0),    ),)candidates = (    :hours => (        :lognormal => LogNormalFamily(starts=(location=log(2.0), log_scale=log(0.8))),        :weibull => WeibullFamily(starts=(log_shape=0.0, log_scale=log(3.0))),    ),)problem = Elicitation(beliefs, candidates)report = fit(Xoshiro(201), problem, BFGS(); adtype=Optimization.AutoForwardDiff())validation = validate(Xoshiro(202), report)validation

The scales express how much a discrepancy in hours contributes to fitting. Both families can match the two quantiles. Compare the implied chance of a repair lasting longer than an eight-hour shift before choosing a family.

map(report.results) do result    (; id=result.id, exceeds_shift=evaluate(Probability(8, Inf), result.measure).value)end
((id = (hours, :lognormal, 1), exceeds_shift = 0.052924245035543264), (id = (hours, :weibull, 1), exceeds_shift = 0.04271672065540793))

The densities show where each family puts its mass around the common quantiles. The conditional completion rate shows how the chance of finishing in the next short interval changes as a repair remains open. It is the density divided by the probability of still being open.

mkpath("elicitation-output")density = priorplot(report, :hours; bounds=(0, 14), xlabel="Repair time (hours)")vlines!(content(density[1, 1]), [2, 6]; color=:gray, linestyle=:dash)save("elicitation-output/repair-density.svg", density)

Here the engineer chooses the LogNormal to represent multiplicative variation in the time needed to diagnose, access, and repair the pump.

choice = select(    report,    :hours,    (:lognormal, 1);    validation,    rationale="The LogNormal represents multiplicative variation in repair duration.",)measure(choice)
